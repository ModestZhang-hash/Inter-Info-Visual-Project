Reading the 3D view

The camera rotates an orthographic view of M, E and L; values are scaled independently to the displayed axis ranges. Lower values are better on every axis, regardless of camera orientation. Dashed gold rings show full-space non-dominance. Only comparison runs are shown; add removed runs again from Matrix or the list. Rotation does not change metrics or Pareto labels. Hover a point for full-precision metrics and overlapping run IDs. Use the list to inspect identical vectors. Focus the plot and use arrow keys to rotate, +/− to zoom, or Home to reset; camera buttons are also available.
